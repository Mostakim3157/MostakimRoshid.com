About

Business graduate who ended up living inside the ERP.

I'm Mostakim Roshid, a BBA graduate in Banking & Insurance from the University of Chittagong. I work at Kolapata Foods Ltd, an integrated food production and retail company in Sonargaon, Narayanganj, where I sit between business operations and the ERP system.
