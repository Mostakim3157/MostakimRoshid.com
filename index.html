About

Business graduate who ended up living inside the ERP.

I'm Mostakim Roshid, a BBA graduate in Banking & Insurance from the University of Chittagong. I work at Kolapata Foods Ltd, an integrated food production and retail company in Sonargaon, Narayanganj, where I sit between business operations and the ERP system.

My day-to-day is solving real operational problems: why stock valuation doesn't match, how landed costs should be spread across an import shipment, how cartons, polys and pieces should convert, and what management needs to see every morning.

I'm studying for ACCA and deepening my Power BI and SQL skills. My long-term goal is to work as an ERP and business solutions consultant.
